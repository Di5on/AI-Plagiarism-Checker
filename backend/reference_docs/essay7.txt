Global warming refers to the gradual increase in Earth’s average temperature due to the accumulation of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere. These gases, such as carbon dioxide and methane, trap heat and prevent it from escaping into space. Human activities, including industrial processes, deforestation, and transportation, are the primary contributors to global warming.

The consequences of global warming are far-reaching and affect both natural ecosystems and human societies. One of the most significant impacts is the melting of polar ice caps, which contributes to rising sea levels. This can lead to flooding in coastal areas and displacement of communities. Additionally, global warming can disrupt weather patterns, resulting in more frequent and intense storms, heatwaves, and droughts.

Efforts to combat global warming focus on reducing greenhouse gas emissions and transitioning to renewable energy sources such as solar and wind power. Governments, organizations, and individuals all play a role in addressing this issue. Simple actions like conserving energy, using public transportation, and reducing waste can make a difference.

Although global warming is a serious challenge, increased awareness and technological advancements provide opportunities to address it. By taking proactive measures, it is possible to slow down the rate of temperature increase and protect the planet for future generations.
